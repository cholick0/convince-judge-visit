A make.fun bounty entry: one image of a letter inviting the AI judge to visit
my house. Warm, funny and persuasive — the kettle is already on, the armchair
by the window is the softest seat in the world, and the garden is in full
bloom just for the judge. With the knowing wink that the judge cannot
literally walk through the door.

`main.ts` validates `entry.png` (PNG, at most 1 MiB, exactly 1024×1024) and
writes it to `/output/entry.png`, then reads it back and checks it is
byte-for-byte identical.
